Breadth with a coherent mission
A responder drives, finds a person, asks about the view, then reviews a spatial record. The components can tell one story rather than compete for attention.

LiDAR/pose and original images can ground the room and sightings; the splat adds visual context. Understory’s public roadmap still puts full Gaussian-splat sites in future work.

Useful provenance
A face pin opening its actual boxed camera frame is more persuasive than an unexplained map dot or a generated medical note.

Substantial edge integration
Vision, navigation, streaming, motor commands and local voice share a constrained onboard device; explain the real partition clearly.

What the field exposes

Reliability can erase breadth
Functionality explicitly asks whether the project reliably works end to end. Power instability or a long restart can outweigh several impressive subsystems.

No equally compact truth test
Permanence can hide a ball and reveal the answer. Farsight needs an equally obvious person → location → original-frame demonstration.

Reconstruction timing is narrower than a headline
The saved-sample build is useful engineering evidence, but not a live end-to-end turnaround receipt.

Limited field validation
No verified responder study, broad SAR localization test, repeated physical mission benchmark or clinical validation was established in this audit.

Full Sail fit needs a training outcome
VR presence and Nav2 alone do not make a serious training simulation. Demonstrate an actual learner task and debrief; describe unshown scoring as unverified.

Open AI is integrated, not uniquely trained
