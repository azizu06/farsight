# Knight Hacks IX — Hacker's Guide (facts only)

Source: https://knight-hacks.notion.site/334e290ccffe80e39ec7ee1e20a33bae (embedded at https://2026.knighthacks.org/dashboard/guide). Captured 2026-10-10. No project ideas here — rules, prizes, deadlines only.

## Event
- Fri Oct 9 – Sun Oct 11, 2026, UCF main campus. 36 hours, teams of up to 4.
- Closing ceremony Sun 6:00–7:30 PM, SU Cape Florida (stream in Key West Ballroom).

## Deadlines
- **Devpost hard deadline: Sun Oct 11, 10:00 AM.** No late submissions. https://knight-hacks-ix.devpost.com/
- **Final code push: Sun Oct 11, 11:00 AM.** No repo changes after.
- Devpost submission help session: Sun 8:00–9:00 AM, ENG2 102.

## Devpost requirements
- One member creates the team and invites the others. Anyone missing from the Devpost team gets no prize.
- Demo video attached.
- Opt into each sponsor/category prize you want (Tracks and Overall are automatic).
- **Public** GitHub repo link. Private or missing repo = disqualified.
- Use the same email you registered with (for post-event feedback).
- Recommended: screenshots/visuals, detailed write-up.

## Judging
- Sun Oct 11, 12:00–4:00 PM. Claim the project on the Hacker Dashboard (email arrives first); appointments (time, room, track) show on the dashboard.
- Per team: 2 min setup, **6 min judging + Q&A**, 2 min teardown. Hard stop.
- At least 1 member must attend; all may present.
- Must attend the General room to be judged for General prizes, **and** each opted-in sponsor's room.
- Missing an appointment without notice = DQ from that challenge **and all General tracks**. Reschedule with an organizer beforehand.
- Rubric: https://knight-hacks.notion.site/Judging-Rubric-38ae290ccffe80618aaac0649d791591 (baseline for all projects; top scorers sorted into General categories).

## Sponsor challenges (prizes per team member)
Judged in each sponsor's own room; opt in on Devpost. Text below is the sponsor brief, condensed.

- **NVIDIA: Best Use of Jetson Nano** (1st-3rd: Jetson Orin Nano + swag). Make Jetson capabilities *central*: real-time computer vision, robotics, local AI assistant, cameras + sensors together.
- **Full Sail: Pathways & Perception** (1st: MSI Vector). A serious game or training simulation that uses pathfinding, face detection, or both to help someone reach a goal, practice a skill, or respond to a scenario. The intelligent system must matter; explain who it helps; show it works. 2D/3D, any engine.
- **Encompass: Data Capturing Hardware** (1st $300 / 2nd $150 / 3rd $75 AI credits). Hardware that captures meaningful real-world data (sensors, cameras, embedded) and turns it into something useful. Judged on creativity, execution, how well the hardware captures and uses data.
- **Hugging Face: Best Use of Open Source AI** (1st $500 / 2nd $300 / 3rd $200 credits; $20 credits per hacker). Text, vision, audio, multimodal, agents or robotics with open-source AI from Hugging Face. Judged on creativity, execution, meaningful use of the HF ecosystem.
- NextEra Energy: SPOT Management Dashboard (internship interview). Turn Boston Dynamics SPOT mission data (their kit zips) into a readable live and post-mission view: locations, timestamps, readings, anomaly flags.
- OneEthos: Innovation for Good. Local environmental problem, measurable impact.
- BNY: Financial Services. Improve a financial-industry process.
- Figma: Best Design (UI/UX, accessibility). Adobe: Prettiest Hack (visual style).
- AMD: Best Use of AMD (FSR4 or ROCm, Radeon Cloud). Databricks: Best Use of Databricks. Neon: Best Use of Neon (Postgres backend).
